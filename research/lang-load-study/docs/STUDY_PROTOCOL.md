# Study protocol (draft for mentor review)

## Research questions
1. **Primary.** Does a clue written in a person's less comfortable language (L2)
   slow visual search compared with the same clue in their most comfortable
   language (L1)?
2. Does that L2 cost differ between novices (high school students) and experts
   (radiologists), and between a familiar-for-everyone image (search scene) and a
   domain image (scan)?
3. **Exploratory.** Do age and years of education moderate the L2 cost?

## Hypotheses
- H1: Search is slower and rated as more effortful with L2 clues.
- H2: Radiologists find the planted target in scans faster than novices
  (or, following the invisible-gorilla finding, possibly slower, because they search
  for clinical findings rather than an unexpected object). Both outcomes are informative.
- H3 (exploratory): The L2 cost is smaller for people with more education.

## Why this is framed as a presentation effect, not a trait claim
Earlier claims of a general "bilingual advantage" have often failed to replicate.
This study does not compare bilinguals with monolinguals. It compares the same
bilingual person under two presentation conditions, so each person is their own control.

## Participants
- Inclusion: reads both English and Turkish (self-rated 3+ on 1-7 in each).
- Novices: high school students, with parent/guardian permission for minors.
- Experts: radiologists and residents.
- Optional additional groups: PhD students, professors (keep exploratory).

## Sample size (planning estimate)
- Within-person language effect: ~30-40 people per group gives good power for a
  medium effect with 12 trials per condition per task.
- Radiologists are scarce; 15-30 is realistic and still informative for the
  within-person effect. Final numbers to be set with mentor guidance.

## Measures
Time to correct click; found vs. timed out (60 s); false clicks; mental effort
(Paas 1-9) per picture; overall difficulty; attention check; background
questionnaire (group, age, years of education, years reading medical images, first
language, age of L2 acquisition, reading proficiency in each language, most
comfortable reading language). No names, emails, or IP addresses are stored.

## Exclusions (decided in advance)
Incomplete sessions; failed attention check; self-rated reading below 3/7 in
either language; response times under 200 ms; trials whose page was reloaded
before answering (the image had already been seen).
